About the Institute

Preserving creative knowledge with care and purpose.

INSTITUTO ANTONIO HENRIQUE AMARAL is a private association whose registered activities include cultural and artistic organizations, libraries, and archives. From our home in Vila Madalena, we value the role that cultural resources play in education, research, reflection, and community life.

Our approach is thoughtful and collaborative. We recognize that art, records, and collections are more than objects: they are points of connection between people, ideas, history, and the future.

Our purpose

To encourage meaningful engagement with cultural and artistic knowledge while supporting responsible access to library and archival resources.
